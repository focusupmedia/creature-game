Privacy Policy

Pocket Grove by Focus Up Media · Last updated October 8, 2026

The short version

  • Pocket Grove has no accounts, no chat and no way to message other players.
  • Your game is saved on your device. If you choose, it is also saved to Game Center (iPhone/iPad) or Google Play Games (Android).
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Ads are shown by Google AdMob, and purchases are handled by Apple or Google.

Information the game keeps

Your progress (creatures, items, coins and settings) is stored on your device. We do not run our own servers and do not receive your save.

Cloud save, leaderboards and achievements

If you sign in to Game Center (Apple) or Google Play Games (Google), your save, scores and achievements are stored with that service under your account. Their use of this information is covered by Apple's and Google's privacy policies.

Friend codes

The game can make a friend code that contains the keeper name you choose, your keeper level, how many creatures you have found and a few of your pets. It is only shared if you copy or share it yourself. Please don't use your real full name as your keeper name.

Advertising

Pocket Grove shows optional video ads that you choose to watch for rewards. These ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address and general information about your device and how ads are used, in order to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, you will be asked for consent first, and on iPhone and iPad you can choose not to allow tracking. You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We use RevenueCat to check that a purchase is valid. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and the purchase receipt, but no payment card details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you allow them, the game schedules gentle reminders on your device (for example, when an egg is ready). They are created on your phone and are not sent from a server. You can turn them off in the game's Settings.

Children

Pocket Grove is made for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has shared personal information with us, contact us and we will help remove it.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the save from your device. To remove a cloud save, delete it in Game Center or Google Play Games, or use "Start over" in the game's Settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact Focus Up Media through the Pocket Grove page on the App Store or Google Play.
